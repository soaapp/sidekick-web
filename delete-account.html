Delete Your Account

Last updated: May 6, 2026

App: Sidekick AI
Developer: Jay Jahanzad

You can delete your Sidekick account and all associated data at any time. We provide two ways to do this.

In-app deletion (recommended)

  1. Open Sidekick on your device
  2. Go to Settings
  3. Scroll to Account and tap Delete Account
  4. Confirm the two-step prompt

Account deletion runs immediately. You'll be signed out and your account, along with all stored data, is removed from our servers.

Email request

If you can't access the app (e.g. uninstalled, locked out), email hello@yoursidekick.ca from the email address tied to your Sidekick account with the subject "Delete my Sidekick account." We'll process the deletion within 7 days and confirm by reply.

What gets deleted

When you delete your account, the following are permanently removed from our servers:

  • Your account profile (email, name, sign-in credentials)
  • All goals, tasks, todos, time blocks, and life areas you created
  • All calendar events you created within Sidekick
  • Energy logs, journal entries, mood data
  • Reward history and earned reward redemptions
  • Streak history and goal completion records
  • Notification preferences and reminder configurations
  • App usage telemetry tied to your account
  • Any third-party connections you authorized (Google Calendar, Google Tasks)

What is retained, and why

  • Crash reports (Firebase Crashlytics) are retained for 90 days for engineering diagnostics. Crash data is tied to a device installation ID, not your account, so it cannot be linked back to you after deletion.
  • Anonymized aggregate metrics (e.g. "10,000 users opened the app today") may be retained indefinitely; these contain no information that can identify you.
  • Payment records, if you ever made a purchase, may be retained by our payment processor for tax and legal compliance per local regulations. Sidekick does not have control over these retention windows.

Third-party calendars and tasks

Deleting your Sidekick account does not delete events or tasks from Google Calendar, iOS Calendar, or Google Tasks — those live in their respective services. To delete data there, manage it directly in each service.

Questions

Email hello@yoursidekick.ca if you have questions or need help completing the deletion.
